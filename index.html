ShortsCutter

ShortsCutter is a private desktop tool for Windows, made by Jakub Żmuda for his own YouTube channels. It edits videos locally (subtitles, voice-over, thumbnails) and keeps them in a review queue. Every video is watched and approved by hand. Only after approval, and only when I click "Upload to YouTube", the video is uploaded to my own channel as a scheduled private upload.

ShortsCutter uses YouTube API Services. By using ShortsCutter you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

ShortsCutter is not publicly available and has no other users.

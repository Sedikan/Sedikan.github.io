Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

ShortsCutter is a private desktop application used only by its author, Jakub Żmuda, to upload approved videos to YouTube channels that he owns. This policy explains what data ShortsCutter accesses and how it is handled.

1. YouTube API Services

ShortsCutter uses YouTube API Services. By using ShortsCutter you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

2. Data we access

When a channel is connected, ShortsCutter requests these Google permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload approved videos and set their thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly: only to read the connected channel's ID and name, so the right channel is shown in the app.

ShortsCutter does not read comments, analytics, subscribers, other users' data or any other YouTube data.

3. How data is used

The data is used only to upload videos that were approved by hand to the connected channel, with the title, description, tags and scheduled publish time chosen in the app.

4. Storage

ShortsCutter stores on the local computer only: the OAuth refresh token, the connected channel's ID and name, and the IDs of uploaded videos. Nothing is stored on any server. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. ShortsCutter has no analytics and no cookies.

5. Deleting data and revoking access

  • In ShortsCutter: open the channel settings and click "Disconnect channel". This revokes the token at Google and deletes it from the computer.
  • In your Google account: you can revoke ShortsCutter's access at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

All stored data can also be deleted by removing the ShortsCutter workspace folder. Stored data is deleted within 7 days of a request sent to the contact e-mail below.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
